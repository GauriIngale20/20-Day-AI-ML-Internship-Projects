# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Findings and Observations

## Project Overview
This report presents the Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of the employee dataset containing 99 records and 8 columns.

## 1. Age Distribution
- Total employees: 99
- Average age: 32.24 years
- Minimum age: 22 years
- Maximum age: 43 years
- Median age: 31 years

**Observation:** Most employees fall within the age range of 26 to 37 years.

## 2. Department-wise Employee Count
| Department | Employee Count |
|---|---:|

## 3. Salary Distribution
- Average salary: ₹47,948.23
- Minimum salary: ₹25,000
- Median salary: ₹46,375
- Maximum salary: ₹74,750

**Observation:** Employee salaries vary across the dataset, with an average salary of approximately ₹47,948.

## 4. Salary Across Departments
**Observation:** The salary box plot helps compare salary ranges, median salaries, and possible outliers across different departments.

## 5. Correlation Analysis
| Variables | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| Age and Salary | 0.05 |
| Age and Experience | -0.01 |

## Conclusion
The EDA process helped understand employee age, salary, department distribution, and relationships between numerical variables. Data cleaning and visualization made the dataset more suitable for analysis.
